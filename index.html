Chiply

A simple app for poker home games.

Chiply helps you run a poker home game. Track players, buy-ins, and chip counts. See who pays whom at the end. All game data stays on your device.

Privacy Policy Support

What Chiply does

  • Tracks players, buy-ins, and chip counts for a home poker game.
  • Calculates who pays whom when the game ends.
  • Stores all game data only on your device. There is no account and no server.
  • Lets you take or pick photos of chip stacks.
  • Has an optional "Count chips with AI" feature. This feature sends photos to OpenAI, using your own OpenAI API key. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • Lets you export your game history as JSON, CSV, or a ZIP file, through the iOS share sheet.
